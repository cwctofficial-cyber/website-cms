01 / WHO WE ARE

Small actions.
Lasting impact.

Coimbatore Wildlife Conservation Trust (CWCT) is a community-focused trust committed to wildlife conservation, environmental awareness and responsible stewardship of nature.

Working with local communities and conservation partners, we support field awareness, wildlife monitoring initiatives and practical actions that help people and nature coexist.
